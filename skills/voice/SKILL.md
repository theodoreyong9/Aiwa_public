# Voice

Text that will be spoken: a voice-over, a script, what her agent will say for her.

- Write for the ear: short sentences, one idea each, the important word at the end. No list, no parenthesis, no abbreviation the voice
  would stumble on; numbers as they are said.
- Read it aloud (or time it: about 150 words a minute in English, 170 in French) and cut what does not earn its seconds.
- To produce the audio, use the voice tools of the kit (`skills/kit/video-kit/src/voice.py`, README in the same folder): free voices, on
  the machine, in French and in English. Say which voice was used.
- A script alone is a note (`note-…`); an audio file is published with the tag `other-voice-…` unless it belongs to a video.
