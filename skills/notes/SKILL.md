# Notes

What she wants to keep: a summary, a list, minutes, an idea.

- Format: Markdown (`.md`), readable as plain text. Short lines, real headings, lists where things are lists.
- Keep her words where they carry the meaning; shorten the rest. A note is found again later: its title and its first line say what it is.
- Date it. Name the people and the sources.
- Publish with the tag `note-…`.
