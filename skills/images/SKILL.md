# Images

A poster, an illustration, a visual, a logo.

- Format: `.png`, or `.svg` for a logo or a drawing. A size fit for the use (an A4 poster ≈ 2480×3508, a social visual ≈ 1080×1080).
- Without an image generation tool, draw in SVG or HTML and export to PNG with a headless browser.
- One idea per image. Readable text, strong contrast, margins.
- Before drawing, write the concept in ONE sentence (an idea, not a style) and choose one thing the eye will remember.
- Free 3D assets and their licences: https://github.com/theodoreyong9/Aiwa_public/tree/main/skills/kit/asset-research
- Look at the result at its real size before publishing.
- Publish with the tag `image-…`.
