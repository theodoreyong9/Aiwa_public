# Slides

A presentation to show or to send.

- Format: `.pdf` (one slide per page, 16:9) unless she asks for `.pptx`. A deck that must be opened on a phone can also be a single HTML
  page with one slide per screen (then follow the page skill, and publish it as `slides-…`).
- One message per slide, said by its title. Few words, large; a figure or a picture beats a paragraph.
- A deck tells a story: where we are, what is at stake, what is proposed, what happens next. Cut every slide the story does not need.
- Render every slide to an image and look at them all in a row before publishing: alignment, contrast, nothing cut.
- Publish with the tag `slides-…`.
